Why Facebook Crops Your Cover & How to Fix It

🖥️ On Desktop / PC

Facebook displays at an ultra-wide 820 × 312 px ratio (~2.63:1). Facebook cuts off roughly 24px from the top and 24px from the bottom (48px each at 2x resolution).

📱 On Mobile Devices

Facebook displays at a taller 640 × 360 px ratio (16:9). Facebook cuts off roughly 90px from the left and 90px from the right (180px each at 2x resolution).

🎯 The Golden Solution: 1640 × 720 px Canvas

By designing on a 1640 × 720 px base canvas and placing your crucial text, logos, and faces inside the central 1280 × 624 px Safe Zone, your cover is guaranteed to look intentional and uncut on every single screen!
